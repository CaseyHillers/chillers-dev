## Just do it (Even if you know you're going to get it wrong)

I watch AI do thing sspectacularly wrong every day, yet I wasn't giving myself the same freedom to experiment.

I spent the past 2 years living in my builder grade house with sterile white walls and Millenial gray accessories. I mulled what the perfect colors
would be to paint every room. In November, I finally took the push to paint my primary bedroom lemongrass which
is a yellow-green. I had never painted interiors before, and was worried it was going to look bad if I did it.
I really wanted to hire someone to do my whole house, but it's hard for me to see the whole picture, so I need
to make incremental progress. After spending a week struggling to paint my bedroom, I finished it. I'm really
proud of the results, but I did make a mess! I fell off the my step ladder at 6 AM and spilled paint drops
over one of the walls, and I stained my carpet in a few places.

However, it was the reminder I needed: Just do it. If the color was bad, I could repaint. If I spilled on the trim, I could touch it up. The stakes were lower than my anxiety made them feel.

## Impact of AI

Working at an AI startup this year made me realize that LLMs are fundamentally reshaping my career. I do not know if I will be completely replaced, but I suspect that in the next few years, the profession of "Software Engineer" will look radically different. Even at a slower enterprise company I find myself managing AI systems more than writing the code myself.

To me, AI is less about autocomplete and more about passive scale. I now routinely have multiple AI agents running simultaneously to tackle different parts of a large project.

If AI data centers are this century's Manhattan Project, I hope humanity harvests the benefits. The idea of throwing money at innovation to solve the world's toughest problems is intriguing. It feels like science fiction to imagine AI systems creating the equivalent of millions of PhD level researchers overnight to find cures for cancer, but in 2025, that future feels closer than ever.

On a pop culture note, AI has officially dominated the "mindshare" of my social circle. For the first time, my non-tech friends are the ones initiating conversations about tech. It has moved from a niche interest to a common dinner table topic.
